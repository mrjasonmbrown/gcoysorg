Our Mission

A Second Chance
To Succeed

The Georgia Council of Youth Sports, Inc. (GCoYS) is dedicated to empowering youth aged 10–18 who face mental health challenges and criminal backgrounds by providing a supportive, second-chance redemption program through sports, mentorship, and life skills development.


We believe in the power of teamwork, discipline, and compassion to break cycles, restore hope, and create a path to a better tomorrow.


Our Vision

To build a generation of confident, resilient, and purpose-driven leaders who thrive in their communities, careers, and personal lives.

About Us

More Than a Program.
A Pathway to Purpose.

Georgia Council of Youth Sports (GCOYS) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in the Atlanta metro area, committed to transforming the lives of youth and young adults ages 10–21 through sports, mentorship, and opportunity.

We exist for the young people who are often overlooked — those navigating limited resources, academic challenges, or difficult environments. At GCOYS, we don't just provide programs — we create structured pathways that lead to real outcomes.

Because we believe every young person deserves more than a chance…
They deserve a system designed for their success.

What We Offer

Our Core Programs

Four pillars that equip young people with the tools, community, and confidence to build successful, independent lives.

🏈

Youth Sports Development

Flag football, soccer, and basketball leagues teaching teamwork, discipline, and physical wellness. Seasonal clinics, camps, and tournaments for all skill levels.

🤝

Mentorship & Behavioral Support

One-on-one and group mentorship pairing youth with adult role models who provide guidance, emotional resilience training, and peer leadership development.

📚

Education & Life Skills

GED prep, vocational training, career readiness, resume building, interview coaching, and workshops on conflict resolution, financial literacy, and personal growth.

🌱

Community Engagement

Food and clothing drives, volunteer service projects, and community initiatives that empower participants to give back and strengthen their neighborhoods.

Vision for the Future

Transforming
Metro Atlanta

GCoYS is building toward a future where every at-risk youth in metro Atlanta has access to the tools and community they need to thrive.

🎯

Serve 500+ youth within the next five years, providing tools and opportunities to break the cycle of incarceration.

📍

Expand to rural counties surrounding metro Atlanta, increasing access to mental health services, education, and career opportunities.

🤲

Grow community partnerships with juvenile courts, schools, and businesses to ensure sustainable, comprehensive support for youth.

🏛️

Secure a community wellness center to serve as the primary hub for all programs, sports, mentorship, and services.
