Coaching expectations

What we expect

  • Be a positive role model in your words, actions, and attitude.
  • Put player safety, development, and fun ahead of winning.
  • Teach the rules and good sportsmanship, and show respect for referees.
  • Encourage every player and give each girl a fair chance to play and learn.
  • Coach with positive instruction. Never use ridicule, threats, or humiliation.
  • Report injuries, safety concerns, and conduct problems to league staff.

Read the full TCFFL Code of Conduct.

Player safety

Safety first

  • Remove a player from play if she shows signs of a concussion or heat illness. The league may require clearance from a health care provider before she returns.
  • Make sure every player has water and breaks, especially on hot days.
  • Check that players wear the equipment the league requires.
  • Know where each player’s emergency contact information is kept, and report every injury to league staff the same day.
