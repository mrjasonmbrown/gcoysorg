More than a game

What every player gets

TCFFL uses flag football to give girls a place to compete, grow, and belong. Every season is built around six things that last long after the final whistle.

Teamwork

Players learn to trust each other, communicate on every snap, and win together.

Academic Enrichment

School comes first. We encourage strong habits in the classroom as well as on the field.

Physical Wellness

Speed, agility, and fitness in a safe, non-tackle game that girls of every level can enjoy.

Leadership

Captains, huddles, and big moments help every girl find her voice and confidence.

Life Skills

Discipline, resilience, and sportsmanship that carry over to school, home, and beyond.

Community

Families, coaches, and neighbors come together every Saturday to cheer these girls on.
