TCFFL Code of Conduct

Respect. Sportsmanship. Fun.

TCFFL is built on sportsmanship, respect, and having fun. This code applies to every player, coach, parent, and fan at TCFFL practices, games, and events, so every girl feels safe, supported, and proud to be part of the team.

PlayersCoachesParents & SpectatorsOff the FieldEnforcement
Purpose

Why we have a code

Flag football is about more than the score. TCFFL gives girls in the 6U–14U divisions a safe place to learn the game, grow as teammates and leaders, and build confidence on and off the field. This code spells out what we expect from everyone who is part of that.

For players

Players

  • Treat teammates, opponents, coaches, referees, and volunteers with respect.
  • Play fair, follow the rules, and accept the referees’ calls.
  • No bullying, taunting, trash talk, or rough or unsafe play.
  • Keep your language clean. No abusive or discriminatory words.
  • Come to practices and games on time and ready to give your best.
  • Take care of your uniform, flags, equipment, and the fields we use.
  • Tell a coach or league staff right away if you see unsafe behavior or bullying.
For coaches

Coaches

  • Be a positive role model in your words, actions, and attitude.
  • Put player safety, development, and fun ahead of winning.
  • Teach the rules and good sportsmanship, and show respect for referees.
  • Encourage every player and give each girl a fair chance to play and learn.
  • Coach with positive instruction. Never use ridicule, threats, or humiliation.
  • Follow league rules and safety guidelines.
  • Report injuries, safety concerns, and conduct problems to league staff.
For parents & spectators

Parents & Spectators

  • Cheer positively for every player on both teams.
  • Let coaches coach and referees make the calls.
  • Stay calm and respectful at games and practices. No arguing, yelling, or confrontations.
  • Keep sidelines and spectator areas safe and family-friendly.
  • No alcohol or drugs at league events.
  • Bring concerns to league staff privately and respectfully, never in front of the players.
On and off the field

Off the Field

  • Represent TCFFL and GCoYS with pride at school, at home, online, and in the community.
  • No bullying, harassment, or discrimination, in person or online.
  • Use social media respectfully. Don’t post negative comments about players, coaches, referees, teams, or families.
  • Keep up with schoolwork. School comes first.
  • Support your teammates outside of games and practices, too.
Enforcement

If the code isn’t followed

Everyone in TCFFL is expected to follow this code. A player, parent, coach, or spectator who ignores it may be spoken to, asked to leave an event, or removed from the league at the league’s discretion. Our goal is for every girl to feel safe, supported, and proud to be part of the team. Questions or concerns? Email info@gcoys.org or call 404-483-6378.

At registration

The agreement every family accepts

TCFFL is built on sportsmanship, respect, and having fun. My daughter and I agree to treat players, coaches, referees, volunteers, and families with respect, and to play fairly and follow the rules. We will not use bullying, taunting, rough or unsafe play, or abusive or discriminatory language, on or off the field. As a parent or guardian, I will cheer positively, let coaches coach and referees make the calls, and stay calm and respectful at games and practices. I understand that a player or parent who ignores this code may be spoken to, asked to leave an event, or removed from the league at the league’s discretion. Our goal is for every girl to feel safe, supported, and proud to be part of the team.
