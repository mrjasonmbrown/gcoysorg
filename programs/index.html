04Launching

Harvest in the Park

Food Security Initiative

What:
Youth meals & snacks after GCOYS activities, plus family food boxes & produce
How:
Recurring drive-through & walk-up distributions at parks & community sites
Plus:
Nutrition info, resource referrals & youth volunteer opportunities
Serves:
Youth, working families, seniors, veterans & residents with limited grocery access
Seeking:
Food partners, meal sponsors, a distribution site & start-up funding
