Four programs. One mission.

Pick a program on the form above, or give where it’s needed most and let GCoYS put it to work right away.

TCFFL

Girls’ flag football for 6U–14U. A $125 gift covers one player’s full season registration.

RePath Program

12 Saturdays of mentorship, skill-building, guest speakers, breakfast and lunch for youth ages 13–17.

Harvest in the Park

Youth meals after GCoYS activities, plus family food boxes and fresh produce at parks and community sites.

GCoYS Collaborative

Bringing youth sports and development partners together across South Metro Atlanta.

Good to know

Giving questions

Is my donation secure?
Yes. You pay on Square’s secure checkout page. GCoYS never sees or stores your card number.
Will I get a receipt?
Square emails a receipt right after you give. Keep it with your reference number for your records.
Is it tax-deductible?
The Georgia Council of Youth Sports is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Please consult your tax advisor.
Can my business sponsor a program?
We’d love that. Email msmoak@gcoys.org to talk about sponsorships, food partnerships or in-kind gifts.
